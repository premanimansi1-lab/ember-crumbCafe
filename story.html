OUR STORY

A café for
slow moments.

EMBER & CRUMB is a fictional café concept imagined as a warm, expressive place where food and atmosphere feel equally considered.

01 / THE IDEA

Start
with good.

We believe a café does not need to be complicated to feel special.

It can be a really good cup of coffee, a warm pastry, a table in the sun and enough time to enjoy it. The brand mixes soft hospitality with a playful visual identity.
